Personal work

Vinyl TikTok — @karllovevinyls

Social media · Content creation · Community building

356 Followers
9.7k Likes
20k+ Top video

I wanted to test the waters and use my favourite social media platform, TikTok, to create a new project for myself. The goal is to learn about the TikTok algorithm, micro-trends, and how to drive engagement.

I chose to create content about my vinyl collection. I started collecting earlier this year and thought it might be interesting to post about my records and build a community of vinyl enthusiasts.

The theme is being goofy and relatable. I wanted to use this as a tool to practice public speaking and develop my overall online presence. I believe that, as a marketer, video editing skills are essential. That's why I took this personal project and made it part of my portfolio. I started from zero, and as of today, I have 35 genuine followers and 2.4k likes. These stats are growing daily as I post content weekly. Ultimately, I want to post daily to make sure I stay relevant and consistent.

My most successful video has over 15,000 views. This success was due to capitalising on trendy content — Lady Gaga re-released vinyls, which collectors were sharing around the same time. Additionally, I used trendy audio to make the video cute and relatable.

How do I plan my content ideas?

I was searching for optimal tools when I discovered Notion. It helps me organise all my content ideas and schedule them throughout the calendar. I noticed that this tool can be ideal for marketing campaigns, social media post scheduling, and much more.

For example, my last video had no editing because I wanted my audience to see the raw material, just as I am.

What are my expectations?

The ultimate goal is to grow a genuine community where vinyl collectors can share their opinions, showcase unique pieces, and have fun.
